# Human Review Boundary

[SYNTHETIC — FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY]

> Human Review Required: AI-generated outputs are decision-support artifacts only. A qualified engineer owns final review and approval.

## Publication Classification

Needs review

## Boundary Statement

Codex can help draft engineering workflow artifacts, organize synthetic data, propose tests, format reports, and identify review gaps. Codex does not approve requirements, WCCA results, risk dispositions, validation plans, design decisions, or release decisions.

## Engineer Responsibilities

- Verify formulas and units.
- Verify assumptions and thresholds.
- Verify requirement interpretation.
- Verify risk labels and mitigations.
- Verify validation-method mapping.
- Confirm examples are synthetic or sanitized.
- Approve or reject final conclusions.

## Codex Responsibilities

- Follow the scoped task instructions.
- Keep work inside the approved folder.
- Generate draft artifacts from synthetic examples.
- Preserve human-review language.
- Avoid proprietary data.
- Support validation and documentation workflows.
